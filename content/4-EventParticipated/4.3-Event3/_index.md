# Summary Report: “AWS Workshop / Hands-on Lab on Agentic AI”

### Event Information

- **Event Name:** AWS Workshop / Hands-on Lab on Agentic AI
- **Time:** 09:00 AM, August 3, 2026
- **Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Tower, Ho Chi Minh City
- **Role:** Attendee and hands-on lab participant

### Event Objective

This workshop focused on helping participants understand how to design and implement an Agentic AI system in a practical way, from architecture design to real lab execution. The session emphasized the connection between architecture, security, and hands-on implementation.

### Main Content

During the workshop, I was introduced to core concepts such as Agentic AI, workflow design, tool integration, and multi-layer system security. The hands-on portion helped me understand how an agent works in practice rather than only at a conceptual level.

### Key Takeaways

- Agentic AI is not just prompting; it is also a system design problem.
- Human-in-the-loop is important for safety and control.
- Choosing the right model and designing tools correctly greatly affects cost and performance.

### Personal Experience

This was a highly valuable experience because I not only learned about the technology but also practiced it directly. It helped me deepen my understanding of how an agent is built and operated in real environments, while also strengthening my system design thinking for future projects.
