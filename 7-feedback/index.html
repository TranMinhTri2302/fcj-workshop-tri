Feedback and Suggestions

Overall Evaluation

1. Working Environment

The working environment at FCAJ, especially the office space on the 26th floor of Bitexco Tower, left a very positive impression on me.

  • Workspace: The office is modern, organized, well-lit, and provides a comfortable environment for focused work. The city view is also a nice bonus that makes long working hours feel more pleasant.
  • Team atmosphere: Everyone is friendly, open-minded, and willing to support one another. Whenever I encountered difficulties, I could always reach out to the team without feeling intimidated or pressured.
  • Facilities and equipment: The internet connection is stable, and essentials such as desks, chairs, air conditioning, power outlets, and screens for workshops/events are all well prepared.
  • Location: Being located in the center of District 1 is very convenient for commuting, dining, or having discussions outside working hours.

Overall, the environment is both professional and comfortable. People work seriously, but the atmosphere remains relaxed, approachable, and respectful.

2. Support from Mentor and Admin Team

Throughout my internship, I received excellent support from both my mentor and the admin team.

  • Mentor: My mentor did not simply show me what to do, but also explained the reasoning behind each technical decision. During code reviews or architecture discussions, my mentor often asked guiding questions so that I could think through the problem and explore possible solutions before receiving direct answers. This approach helped me understand the core of each issue instead of just following step-by-step instructions.
  • Admin Team: Questions and administrative matters such as internship/report/blog guidelines, office access arrangements, and equipment support were handled promptly. This allowed me to focus more on the technical side of the internship.

What impressed me the most was my mentor’s mindset: “Try to figure it out first, but never hesitate to ask when you truly need help.” This helped me improve my ability to self-learn, debug independently, and take ownership of my work.

3. Relevance Between the Work and My Field of Study

The Smart Campus Platform project was highly relevant to my background in Computer Science and Cloud Computing. It not only allowed me to apply what I had learned at university, but also exposed me to many practical areas that are not often explored in depth in academic settings.

4. Opportunities for Learning and Skill Development

This internship gave me tremendous opportunities to learn and develop new skills, including the use of project management tools, teamwork, and professional communication in a corporate environment. My mentor and the senior team members also shared valuable real-world experiences that helped me shape a clearer view of my future career path.

5. Culture and Team Spirit

The working culture at FCAJ made me feel that I was not just an intern there to learn, but truly a member of the team.

  • Everyone respects each other’s ideas, even when those ideas are still incomplete.
  • Interns have the opportunity to join planning sessions, retrospectives, architecture reviews, and technical discussions.
  • Senior members are always willing to help, while fresh perspectives from interns are also genuinely valued.
  • Asking questions, admitting uncertainty, or making mistakes is not seen as something negative; instead, it is considered a natural part of the learning process.

Additional Questions

If you were to recommend this internship to your friends, would you do so? Why?

Yes, absolutely — I would confidently recommend it and rate the experience 10/10.

The reason is that this program does not just assign simulated exercises to interns; it gives them the opportunity to work on real technical problems involving system design, data processing, security, monitoring, CI/CD, and operational optimization.

Here are some of the things I believe FCAJ does especially well:

  • Real-world projects: Interns get hands-on exposure to technologies and workflows that are close to production environments.
  • Dedicated mentorship: Mentors do not simply assign tasks; they help interns understand how to think through technical decisions.
  • Modern tech stack: Serverless, event-driven architecture, AI/ML, cloud security, and data engineering are all highly valuable areas for today’s career paths.
  • A balance between autonomy and support: Interns are encouraged to explore and experiment independently, while still having guidance available whenever needed.
  • Strong community: There are opportunities to engage with community activities, technical events, and professionals who are actively working in the AWS/cloud ecosystem.

That said, I believe future interns should prepare some basic knowledge beforehand, such as Git, HTTP, JSON, Python or JavaScript, as well as foundational concepts of EC2, S3, Lambda, and IAM. If someone is completely new to cloud computing, the initial stage may feel a bit overwhelming.

Would you like to continue being involved with the program in the future?

Yes. If there is a suitable opportunity, I would be very happy to continue contributing to FCAJ in one of the following roles:

  • Alumni Mentor: Supporting, sharing experience with, and guiding future intern cohorts.
  • Part-time/Contract Contributor: Continuing to contribute to areas such as AI Assistant, Security, or Mobile App development.
  • Full-time: If there is a position aligned with my career direction as a Cloud Engineer, AI Engineer, or Solutions Architect.

Personal Reflection After the Internship

This internship served as a very clear bridge between academic knowledge and real-world industry work. Before joining, I had a basic understanding of what Lambda was and some fundamental cloud concepts. After the internship, I not only learned how to use cloud services, but also how to think when designing a serverless system: how to make it scalable, observable, fault-tolerant, secure, and cost-efficient.

What I value most is the patience of the mentors, the openness of the team, and FCAJ’s willingness to entrust interns with real responsibilities. Long event days, feedback sessions during code and diagram reviews, and architecture discussions were all meaningful experiences that helped me grow significantly.

In particular, I would like to express my sincere gratitude to Mr. Nguyễn Gia Hưng (SA Director, AWS Vietnam). Through the FCAJ community that he has led, I found an ideal environment to develop both my mindset and technical skills. His practical insights and wholehearted support helped me reflect on myself, recognize areas where I need improvement, and stay committed to continuous growth every day.
